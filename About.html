ABOUT CERTILOCK

Secure your certificate process.

CertiLock helps learners create a clear, consistent certificate of clearance for their course work. Register and log in with your own account, provide your certificate details, and download a PDF for your records.

Courses

Certificates are available for Backend Development, Frontend Development, Cybersecurity, UI/UX Design, and Data Analysis.

How it works

  1. Enter your name, registration number, email, course, purpose, and date.
  2. Generate and download your certificate as a PDF.
  3. Each account can create one certificate and edit it once online. Registration number and email stay locked.
